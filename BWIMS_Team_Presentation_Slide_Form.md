# BWIMS Team Presentation Slide Form

## Purpose

This document assigns each BWIMS presentation slide to a team member and provides a consistent format for completing it.

## Presentation format

- Use 16:9 widescreen layout.
- Use one clear message per slide.
- Include no more than three short bullet points.
- Use a slide title of at least 35pt.
- Use body text between 18pt and 24pt.
- Include at least one screenshot, diagram, test result or other piece of evidence.
- Put detailed explanations in speaker notes.

## Slide completion form

Each member must complete their assigned slide using this structure:

### Slide title

Write one clear takeaway.

### What we completed

- Completed item one
- Completed item two
- Completed item three

### Why it matters

Write one short sentence explaining the value of the work.

### Evidence

Add a screenshot, diagram, test result, GitHub issue or working demonstration.

### Next step

State what will happen next according to the Week 6–15 Gantt chart.

### Owner

Write the responsible member’s name.

## Required submission

Every member must provide:

1. Their completed slide.
2. At least one real piece of evidence.
3. A short explanation lasting approximately one minute.
4. Their next task according to the Gantt chart.

## Submission request

Please complete your assigned slide and share it with the team for review.
