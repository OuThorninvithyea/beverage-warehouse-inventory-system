BWIMS barcode test sheet

Print this page at 100% scale (no "fit to page"), or open it on a second screen. In the Receive, Pick, Transfer or Adjust form press Scan, then scan a shelf label (section 1) followed by a product (section 2). In Transfer the first shelf is the source and the second the destination. Section 3 holds real retail products that are not in the catalog: in Receive, a manager or admin gets "Create product" with the details filled in from Open Food Facts. Sections 1 and 2 match the development seeder, and the bars are drawn from the digits below them, so they cannot drift.
