NOTES
-----
- The 884 (Cambodia) and 20x (internal use) values are invented for this
  project. They are structurally valid and scan correctly, but no physical
  product carries them.
- A wrong-check-digit value cannot be drawn as a legitimate EAN-13 barcode,
  so the rejection cases are type-in only.
- Browsers block camera access over plain HTTP except on localhost. Use the
  laptop webcam on localhost, or serve over HTTPS to scan with a phone.
